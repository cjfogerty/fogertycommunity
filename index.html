Easy Home Practice Ideas

10–15 minutes of play is plenty. Keep it fun, not formal.

  • Dribbling: Walk the ball around the yard without letting it “escape.”
  • Stopping: Red light / green light while dribbling.
  • Kicking: Soft targets into a laundry basket or against a fence.
  • Coordination: Hop over the ball, balance on one foot holding the ball.
Praise effort constantly: “I love how hard you tried!” Kids this age learn best when it feels like play and every player has a ball as much as possible.
🌱

What We’re Really Working On

Physical

Running, balance, changing direction, basic kicking & dribbling

Social & Emotional

Listening, taking turns, encouraging others, handling small frustrations

Cognitive

Following simple directions, spatial awareness

Biggest Goal

That every kid finishes the season wanting to play again

There is no focus on positions, tactics, or winning. All the kids will chase the ball — that’s completely normal and good at this age. Every child develops at their own pace.
