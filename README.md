## Sample Weekly Practice Agenda (~60 min)
1. **Arrival Free Play** (5–10 min) – Kids just run, kick, and explore as they arrive. Parents can join in!
2. **Ball Mastery Games** (10 min) – Toe taps, inside/outside dribbling, red light/green light.
3. **Fun Movement Games** (10–15 min) – Sharks & minnows, freeze tag with ball, treasure hunt (dribble to cones).
4. **Small-Sided Play** (15–20 min) – 2v2 or 3v3 "matches" with lots of high-fives and zero pressure.
5. **Cheer & Cool Down** – Team cheer, high-fives, water.

*Every player should have a ball as much as possible. Short activities, lots of movement, pure fun.*

## Home Practice Tips (10–15 minutes is perfect)
Keep it playful – this is not "drills," it's playtime with a ball!

- **Dribbling:** Walk the ball around the yard/house without it "escaping."
- **Stopping:** Red light / green light while dribbling.
- **Kicking:** Soft kicks into a laundry basket, against a fence, or to a parent.
- **Coordination:** Hop over the ball, balance on one foot while holding the ball, jump and land softly.
- **Listening game:** "Freeze!" when coach/parent says stop.

Praise effort constantly: "I love how hard you tried!" or "Great job keeping the ball close!"

## Developmental Milestones for U5 Soccer
At this age we focus on:
- **Physical:** Running, balance, coordination, changing direction, basic kicking/dribbling.
- **Social/Emotional:** Taking turns, listening to simple instructions, encouraging teammates, handling small frustrations.
- **Cognitive:** Following 1–2 step directions, spatial awareness ("stay in the area").
- **Love of the Game:** The #1 goal is that every kid finishes the season wanting to play again next year.

There is **no** focus on positions, tactics, winning, or perfect technique. Kids this age are egocentric (they all chase the ball – that's normal and good!).

Every child develops at their own pace. Some will be natural dribblers; others will still be figuring out how to kick without falling. Both are perfect.

## Parent Engagement Ideas
- Arrive 5 minutes early so kids can settle and play.
- Positive sideline energy only – cheer for effort and fun.
- Offer to help with a game activity or organize a simple snack rotation if desired.
- Share photos/videos in a group chat (we'll create one if helpful).
- Practice at home and tell Coach what your child is excited about!
