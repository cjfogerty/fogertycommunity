# Eastern Orthodox parishes — St. Louis metro

We had **zero** Orthodox points on the map. They are on it now (gold markers, `tradition: orthodox` in `churches.json`).

Pew RLS still files Orthodox as **<1%** of St. Louis adults. The 2020 U.S. Religion Census is more useful at the congregation level:

| Body (2020 ARDA, St. Louis metro) | Congregations | Adherents |
|---|---:|---:|
| Greek Orthodox Archdiocese | 3 | 3,400 |
| Serbian Orthodox | 1 | 500 |
| Orthodox Church in America | 4 | 370 |
| Coptic Orthodox (Oriental, not Eastern) | 1 | 226 |
| ROCOR | 4 | 215 |
| Eritrean Orthodox (Oriental) | 1 | 336 |

That is a few thousand adherents, not a megachurch market. Weekly liturgy is smaller than adherent counts — Greek parishes typically see ~25–40% of adherents on a normal Sunday.
