# St. Louis Metro Churches — Attendance & Giving Research

**Last updated:** 2026-09-19  
**Scope:** St. Louis metro area (MO-IL), all denominations + major non-Christian faiths. Sorted by estimated weekly attendance (largest first).  
**Method:** Public sources only — church annual reports, financial statements, news, directories, LCMS stats, Hartford Institute / Outreach data, Pew RLS, UMData, Archdiocese reports, Jewish Federation studies. Figures are approximate and often self-reported. Catholic weekly counts are especially hard to pin down; mosque/synagogue numbers are sparse.

## Market Context (for meta-analysis)
- **Metro population:** ~2.81 million (2024, US Census).
- **Religiously unaffiliated ("nones"):** ~32% of adults (Pew 2023-24 RLS: 3% atheist, 9% agnostic, 20% nothing in particular). Weekly service attendance ~26% (down from 30% in 2014); 50% attend seldom or never.
- **Christian identification:** 62% (down from 75% in 2014). Catholic 22%, Evangelical Protestant 21%, Mainline 13%, Black Protestant ~5%.
- **2020 US Religion Census (metro adherents):** ~1.44 million adherents (~51% of population). Catholic ~597k, Non-denominational ~161k, SBC ~131k, LCMS ~78k, UMC ~67k, National Missionary Baptist ~55k, Muslim ~37k, Jewish (est. ~46k per 2024 study).
- **Archdiocese Mass counts (2025):** 26.5% of registered Catholics attended weekend Mass (up from 25.5% in 2024, 24.3% in 2023); ~4,500 more worshippers than 2024. Still below 2019's 29.3%.
- **UMC Missouri Conference (2024):** Average worship attendance 37,535 (down from 38,485 in 2023); membership 102,258.
- **Implication for market share:** The largest ~10-15 churches capture a disproportionate share of the churched population (consistent with national megachurch data: <1% of churches draw ~17% of Protestant worshippers). Catholic parishes hold the largest raw adherent base but lower per-parish weekly attendance due to many small/merged parishes. Non-Christian faiths (Jewish ~46k, Muslim ~37k) are smaller but highly engaged in some metrics.

## Data gaps & next steps
- Catholic parishes rarely publish weekly Mass counts; Archdiocese does annual October counts but releases aggregates, not per-parish except via FOIA/news.
- Most Protestant megachurches publish attendance in annual reports or Outreach/HIRR; giving is rarer (Waypoint is a strong exception with public weekly budget tracking).
- Synagogue/mosque attendance is rarely published; Jewish study gives household membership (36%) and service attendance (64% at least once/year) but not per-congregation weekly.
- LDS wards are small by design (~150-500 members); activity is high but absolute numbers modest in metro.
- Next: pull Form 990s for nonprofits where available, expand to more LCMS/UMC/Baptist parishes, add a CSV export, and dig into SBC/Assemblies of God side (James River Assembly is the state's largest at ~8,200 but outside metro).
