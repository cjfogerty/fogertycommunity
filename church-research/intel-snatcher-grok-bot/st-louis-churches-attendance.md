# St. Louis Metro Churches — Attendance & Giving Research

**Last updated:** 2026-09-19  
**Scope:** St. Louis metro area (MO-IL), all denominations. Sorted by estimated weekly attendance (largest first).  
**Method:** Public sources only — church annual reports, financial statements, news, directories, LCMS stats, Hartford Institute / Outreach data, Pew RLS. Figures are approximate and often self-reported. Catholic weekly counts are especially hard to pin down.

## Market Context (for meta-analysis)
- **Metro population:** ~2.81 million (2024, US Census).
- **Religiously unaffiliated ("nones"):** ~32% of adults (Pew 2023-24 RLS: 3% atheist, 9% agnostic, 20% nothing in particular). Weekly service attendance ~26% (down from 30% in 2014); 50% attend seldom or never.
- **Christian identification:** 62% (down from 75% in 2014). Catholic 22%, Evangelical Protestant 21%, Mainline 13%, Black Protestant ~5%.
- **2020 US Religion Census (metro adherents):** ~1.44 million adherents (~51% of population). Catholic ~597k, Non-denominational ~161k, SBC ~131k, LCMS ~78k, UMC ~67k, National Missionary Baptist ~55k.
- **Archdiocese Mass counts (2025):** 26.5% of registered Catholics attended weekend Mass (up from 25.5% in 2024, 24.3% in 2023); ~4,500 more worshippers than 2024. Still below 2019's 29.3%.
- **Implication for market share:** The largest ~10-15 churches capture a disproportionate share of the churched population (consistent with national megachurch data: <1% of churches draw ~17% of Protestant worshippers). Catholic parishes hold the largest raw adherent base but lower per-parish weekly attendance due to many small/merged parishes.

## Data gaps & next steps
- Catholic parishes rarely publish weekly Mass counts; Archdiocese does annual October counts but releases aggregates, not per-parish except via FOIA/news.
- Most Protestant megachurches publish attendance in annual reports or Outreach/HIRR; giving is rarer.
- Next: pull Form 990s for nonprofits where available, expand to more LCMS/UMC/Baptist parishes, and add a CSV export.
