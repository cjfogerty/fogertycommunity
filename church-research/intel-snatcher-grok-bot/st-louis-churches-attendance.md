## Data gaps & next steps
- Catholic parishes rarely publish weekly Mass counts; Archdiocese does annual October counts but releases aggregates, not per-parish except via FOIA/news.
- Most Protestant megachurches publish attendance in annual reports or Outreach/HIRR; giving is rarer.
- Next: pull Form 990s for nonprofits where available, expand to more LCMS/UMC/Baptist parishes, and add a CSV export.
