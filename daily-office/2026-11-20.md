2 Incline your ear to me; *  
&emsp;when I call, make haste to answer me,

&emsp;so that I forget to eat my bread.

&emsp;and I wither like the grass.

12 But you, O LORD, endure for ever, *  
&emsp;and your Name from age to age.

13 You will arise and have compassion on Zion,  
for it is time to have mercy upon her; *  
&emsp;indeed, the appointed time has come.

14 For your servants love her very rubble, *  
&emsp;and are moved to pity even for her dust.

15 The nations shall fear your Name, O LORD, *  
&emsp;and all the kings of the earth your glory.

16 For the LORD will build up Zion, *  
&emsp;and his glory will appear.

18 Let this be written for a future generation, *

## The Lessons (BCP p. 84)

### The First Lesson: Malachi 3:1–12

**Reader:** A Reading from the Book of the prophet Malachi.

*In brief:* The Lord announces a messenger who will prepare his way, and then the Lord himself will come suddenly to his temple, like a refiner's fire and fullers' soap, purifying the descendants of Levi until their offerings are pleasing again. God declares that he does not change, and calls his people to return to him, promising to return to them. He rebukes them for robbing him in tithes and offerings, and invites them to bring the full tithe, promising to open the windows of heaven and pour out blessing until it overflows.

*The full NRSV text is in your Bible or an authorized source:* [Malachi 3:1–12 (NRSV)](https://bible.oremus.org/?passage=Malachi+3%3A1-12&version=nrsv)

**Reader:** The Word of the Lord.  
**People:** **Thanks be to God.**

*Silence may be kept.*

### The Second Lesson: Luke 18:1–8

**Reader:** A Reading from the Gospel according to Luke.

*In brief:* Jesus tells his disciples a parable about the need to pray always and not lose heart: a widow keeps pleading before a judge who neither fears God nor respects people, until he grants her justice simply to be rid of her persistence. Jesus says that if even an unjust judge responds, God will surely grant justice to his chosen ones who cry to him day and night, and he asks whether the Son of Man will find faith on earth when he comes.

*The full NRSV text is in your Bible or an authorized source:* [Luke 18:1–8 (NRSV)](https://bible.oremus.org/?passage=Luke+18%3A1-8&version=nrsv)

**Reader:** The Word of the Lord.  
**People:** **Thanks be to God.**

*Silence may be kept.*

## The General Thanksgiving (BCP p. 101)

*Before the close of the Office one or both of the following may be used*

**Officiant and People:**

Almighty God, Father of all mercies,  
we your unworthy servants give you humble thanks  
for all your goodness and loving-kindness  
to us and to all whom you have made.  
We bless you for our creation, preservation,  
and all the blessings of this life;  
but above all for your immeasurable love  
in the redemption of the world by our Lord Jesus Christ;  
for the means of grace, and for the hope of glory.  
And, we pray, give us such an awareness of your mercies,  
that with truly thankful hearts we may show forth your praise,  
not only with our lips, but in our lives,  
by giving up our selves to your service,  
and by walking before you  
in holiness and righteousness all our days;  
through Jesus Christ our Lord,  
to whom, with you and the Holy Spirit,  
be honor and glory throughout all ages. Amen.
