Opening Sentence

Officiant:

One of the sentences for any time (BCP pp. 75–78). Suggested: The Lord is in his holy temple; let all the earth keep silence before him. (Habakkuk 2:20)

Confession of Sin

Officiant:

Let us confess our sins against God and our neighbor.

Silence may be kept.

Officiant and People:

Most merciful God, we confess that we have sinned against you... (full text BCP p. 79)

Officiant:

Almighty God have mercy on you... (absolution, BCP p. 80)

Invitatory: Venite or Jubilate (BCP pp. 80–82)

Psalm 89:1–18 (NRSV)

The Lessons

First Lesson

Habakkuk 2:1–4, 9–20 (NRSV)

Summary: The prophet stands watch and receives a vision of judgment against the proud and violent, yet the righteous live by faith. The Lord is in his holy temple; all the earth keep silence before him.

Canticle 9: Ecce, Deus (The First Song of Isaiah)

After the Canticle: Glory to the Father...

Second Lesson

Luke 16:19–31 (NRSV)

Summary: The parable of the rich man and Lazarus. The rich man, who ignored the poor man at his gate, suffers in Hades while Lazarus is comforted with Abraham. A great chasm separates them. Moses and the prophets are sufficient warning; even one rising from the dead will not convince those who refuse to listen.

Canticle 19: Magna et mirabilia (The Song of the Redeemed)

After the Canticle: Glory to the Father...
