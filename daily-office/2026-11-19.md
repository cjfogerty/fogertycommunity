### The First Lesson: Malachi 2:1–16

**Reader:** A Reading from the Book of the prophet Malachi.

*In brief:* The Lord warns the priests that if they will not honor his name, he will turn their blessings into curses. He recalls his covenant of life and peace with Levi, whose true teaching kept many from iniquity, and contrasts the priests who have caused many to stumble. The prophet then asks why people deal faithlessly with one another, profaning the covenant of their ancestors, and calls them to faithfulness, including to the wife of one's youth, for the Lord hates divorce and violence and asks them to guard their spirit.

*The full NRSV text is in your Bible or an authorized source:* [Malachi 2:1–16 (NRSV)](https://bible.oremus.org/?passage=Malachi+2%3A1-16&version=nrsv)

**Reader:** The Word of the Lord.  
**People:** **Thanks be to God.**

*Silence may be kept.*

### The Second Lesson: Luke 17:20–37

**Reader:** A Reading from the Gospel according to Luke.

*In brief:* Asked by the Pharisees when the kingdom of God is coming, Jesus answers that it does not come with signs to be observed: the kingdom of God is among you. He then tells his disciples about the days of the Son of Man, which will be sudden like lightning, recalling the days of Noah and of Lot, when people went on eating and drinking until the day of judgment. He urges them not to look back, saying whoever tries to save their life will lose it, and whoever loses it will keep it.

*The full NRSV text is in your Bible or an authorized source:* [Luke 17:20–37 (NRSV)](https://bible.oremus.org/?passage=Luke+17%3A20-37&version=nrsv)

**Reader:** The Word of the Lord.  
**People:** **Thanks be to God.**

*Silence may be kept.*

## The General Thanksgiving (BCP p. 101)

*Before the close of the Office one or both of the following may be used*

**Officiant and People:**

Almighty God, Father of all mercies,  
we your unworthy servants give you humble thanks  
for all your goodness and loving-kindness  
to us and to all whom you have made.  
We bless you for our creation, preservation,  
and all the blessings of this life;  
but above all for your immeasurable love  
in the redemption of the world by our Lord Jesus Christ;  
for the means of grace, and for the hope of glory.  
And, we pray, give us such an awareness of your mercies,  
that with truly thankful hearts we may show forth your praise,  
not only with our lips, but in our lives,  
by giving up our selves to your service,  
and by walking before you  
in holiness and righteousness all our days;  
through Jesus Christ our Lord,  
to whom, with you and the Holy Spirit,  
be honor and glory throughout all ages. Amen.
