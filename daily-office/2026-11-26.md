## The Opening Sentence (BCP p. ~64)

*The Officiant begins the service with one or more sentences of Scripture (pp. 61–64), or with “Lord, open our lips” on p. 65.*

**Officiant:** Let the words of my mouth and the meditation of our hearts be acceptable in your sight, O Lord, our strength and our redeemer.   Psalm 19:14

## Confession of Sin (BCP p. 64–65)

*The following Confession of Sin may then be said; or the Office may continue at once with “Lord, open our lips.”*

*The Officiant says to the people*

**Officiant:** Let us confess our sins against God and our neighbor.

*or the longer exhortation (p. 64):* Dearly beloved, we have come together in the presence of Almighty God our heavenly Father, to set forth his praise, to hear his holy Word, and to ask, for ourselves and on behalf of others, those things that are necessary for our life and our salvation. And so that we may prepare ourselves in heart and mind to worship him, let us kneel in silence, and with penitent and obedient hearts confess our sins, that we may obtain forgiveness by his infinite goodness and mercy.

*Silence may be kept.*

*Officiant and People together, all kneeling*

**Officiant and People:**

Most merciful God,  
we confess that we have sinned against you  
in thought, word, and deed,  
by what we have done,  
and by what we have left undone.  
We have not loved you with our whole heart;  
we have not loved our neighbors as ourselves.  
We are truly sorry and we humbly repent.  
For the sake of your Son Jesus Christ,  
have mercy on us and forgive us;  
that we may delight in your will,  
and walk in your ways,  
to the glory of your Name. Amen.

**Priest:** Almighty God have mercy on you, forgive you all your sins through our Lord Jesus Christ, strengthen you in all goodness, and by the power of the Holy Spirit keep you in eternal life. Amen.

### Venite — Psalm 95:1–7 (BCP p. 67)

Come, let us sing to the Lord; *  
&emsp;let us shout for joy to the Rock of our salvation.

Let us come before his presence with thanksgiving *  
&emsp;and raise a loud shout to him with psalms.

For the Lord is a great God, *  
&emsp;and a great King above all gods.

In his hand are the caverns of the earth, *  
&emsp;and the heights of the hills are his also.

The sea is his, for he made it, *  
&emsp;and his hands have molded the dry land.

Come, let us bow down, and bend the knee, *  
&emsp;and kneel before the Lord our Maker.

For he is our God,  
and we are the people of his pasture and the sheep of his hand. *  
&emsp;Oh, that today you would hearken to his voice!

Be joyful in the Lord, all you lands; *  
&emsp;serve the Lord with gladness  
&emsp;and come before his presence with a song.

Know this: The Lord himself is God; *  
&emsp;he himself has made us, and we are his;  
&emsp;we are his people and the sheep of his pasture.

Enter his gates with thanksgiving;  
go into his courts with praise; *  
&emsp;give thanks to him and call upon his Name.

For the Lord is good;  
his mercy is everlasting; *  
&emsp;and his faithfulness endures from age to age.

*The antiphon may be repeated after the Invitatory Psalm.*

### Psalm 147 — Laudate Dominum (BCP p. ~722–723)

1 Hallelujah!  
How good it is to sing praises to our God! *  
&emsp;how pleasant it is to honor him with praise!

2 The LORD rebuilds Jerusalem; *  
&emsp;he gathers the exiles of Israel.

3 He heals the brokenhearted *  
&emsp;and binds up their wounds.

4 He counts the number of the stars *  
&emsp;and calls them all by their names.

5 Great is our LORD and mighty in power; *  
&emsp;there is no limit to his wisdom.

6 The LORD lifts up the lowly, *  
&emsp;but casts the wicked to the ground.

7 Sing to the LORD with thanksgiving; *  
&emsp;make music to our God upon the harp.

8 He covers the heavens with clouds *  
&emsp;and prepares rain for the earth;

9 He makes grass to grow upon the mountains *  
&emsp;and green plants to serve mankind.

10 He provides food for flocks and herds *  
&emsp;and for the young ravens when they cry.

11 He is not impressed by the might of a horse; *  
&emsp;he has no pleasure in the strength of a man;

12 But the LORD has pleasure in those who fear him, *  
&emsp;in those who await his gracious favor.

13 Worship the LORD, O Jerusalem; *  
&emsp;praise your God, O Zion;

14 For he has strengthened the bars of your gates; *  
&emsp;he has blessed your children within you.

15 He has established peace on your borders; *  
&emsp;he satisfies you with the finest wheat.

16 He sends out his command to the earth, *  
&emsp;and his word runs very swiftly.

17 He gives snow like wool; *  
&emsp;he scatters hoarfrost like ashes.

18 He scatters his hail like bread crumbs; *  
&emsp;who can stand against his cold?

19 He sends forth his word and melts them; *  
&emsp;he blows with his wind, and the waters flow.

20 He declares his word to Jacob, *  
&emsp;his statutes and his judgments to Israel.

21 He has not done so to any other nation; *  
&emsp;to them he has not revealed his judgments.  
&emsp;Hallelujah!

*At the end of the Psalms is sung or said*

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit: *  
&emsp;as it was in the beginning, is now, and will be for ever. Amen.

## The Lessons (BCP p. ~69)

*One or two lessons, as appointed, are read, the Reader first saying “A Reading (Lesson) from ____.” A citation giving chapter and verse may be added.*

### The First Lesson: Deuteronomy 26:1–11

**Reader:** A Reading from the Book of Deuteronomy.

*In brief:* When Israel enters the land the Lord gives, the people are to bring the first of all the fruit of the ground to the priest in a basket, and recite their story: a wandering Aramean ancestor, slavery in Egypt, and the Lord who heard their cry and brought them out with a mighty hand. They set the offering before the Lord, bow down, and rejoice in all the good the Lord has given to them, their households, and the resident alien among them.

*The full NRSV text is in your Bible or an authorized source:* [Deuteronomy 26:1–11 (NRSV)](https://bible.oremus.org/?passage=Deuteronomy+26%3A1-11&version=nrsv)

**Reader:** The Word of the Lord.  
**People:** **Thanks be to God.**

*Silence may be kept.*

### Canticle 12: A Song of Creation — Benedicite, omnia opera Domini (BCP p. ~73–74)

*Song of the Three Young Men, 35-65*

*One or more sections of this Canticle may be used. Whatever the selection, it begins with the Invocation and concludes with the Doxology.*

*Invocation*

Glorify the Lord, all you works of the Lord, *  
&emsp;praise him and highly exalt him for ever.

In the firmament of his power, glorify the Lord, *  
&emsp;praise him and highly exalt him for ever.

*I — The Cosmic Order*

Glorify the Lord, you angels and all powers of the Lord, *  
&emsp;O heavens and all waters above the heavens.

Sun and moon and stars of the sky, glorify the Lord, *  
&emsp;praise him and highly exalt him for ever.

Glorify the Lord, every shower of rain and fall of dew, *  
&emsp;all winds and fire and heat.

Winter and Summer, glorify the Lord, *  
&emsp;praise him and highly exalt him for ever.

Glorify the Lord, O chill and cold, *  
&emsp;drops of dew and flakes of snow.

Frost and cold, ice and sleet, glorify the Lord, *  
&emsp;praise him and highly exalt him for ever.

Glorify the Lord, O nights and days, *  
&emsp;O shining light and enfolding dark.

Storm clouds and thunderbolts, glorify the Lord, *  
&emsp;praise him and highly exalt him for ever.

*II — The Earth and its Creatures*

Let the earth glorify the Lord, *  
&emsp;praise him and highly exalt him for ever.

Glorify the Lord, O mountains and hills,  
and all that grows upon the earth, *  
&emsp;praise him and highly exalt him for ever.

Glorify the Lord, O springs of water, seas, and streams, *  
&emsp;O whales and all that move in the waters.

All birds of the air, glorify the Lord, *  
&emsp;praise him and highly exalt him for ever.

Glorify the Lord, O beasts of the wild, *  
&emsp;and all you flocks and herds.

O men and women everywhere, glorify the Lord, *  
&emsp;praise him and highly exalt him for ever.

*III — The People of God*

Let the people of God glorify the Lord, *  
&emsp;praise him and highly exalt him for ever.

Glorify the Lord, O priests and servants of the Lord, *  
&emsp;praise him and highly exalt him for ever.

Glorify the Lord, O spirits and souls of the righteous, *  
&emsp;praise him and highly exalt him for ever.

You that are holy and humble of heart, glorify the Lord, *  
&emsp;praise him and highly exalt him for ever.

*Doxology*

Let us glorify the Lord: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit; *  
&emsp;praise him and highly exalt him for ever.

In the firmament of his power, glorify the Lord, *  
&emsp;praise him and highly exalt him for ever.

### The Second Lesson: John 6:26–35

**Reader:** A Reading from the Gospel according to John.

*In brief:* The crowd seeks Jesus after the feeding of the five thousand, and he tells them not to work for food that perishes but for food that endures to eternal life, which the Son of Man gives. When they ask for a sign like the manna in the wilderness, Jesus says that God gives the true bread from heaven, and declares, “I am the bread of life; whoever comes to me will never be hungry, and whoever believes in me will never be thirsty.”

*The full NRSV text is in your Bible or an authorized source:* [John 6:26–35 (NRSV)](https://bible.oremus.org/?passage=John+6%3A26-35&version=nrsv)

**Reader:** The Word of the Lord.  
**People:** **Thanks be to God.**

*Silence may be kept.*

### Canticle 21: You are God — Te Deum laudamus (BCP p. ~79–80)

You are God: we praise you;  
You are the Lord; we acclaim you;  
You are the eternal Father:  
All creation worships you.  
To you all angels, all the powers of heaven,  
Cherubim and Seraphim, sing in endless praise:  
&emsp;Holy, holy, holy Lord, God of power and might,  
&emsp;heaven and earth are full of your glory.  
The glorious company of apostles praise you.  
The noble fellowship of prophets praise you.  
The white-robed army of martyrs praise you.  
Throughout the world the holy Church acclaims you;  
&emsp;Father, of majesty unbounded,  
&emsp;your true and only Son, worthy of all worship,  
&emsp;and the Holy Spirit, advocate and guide.  
You, Christ, are the king of glory,  
the eternal Son of the Father.  
When you became man to set us free  
you did not shun the Virgin's womb.  
You overcame the sting of death  
and opened the kingdom of heaven to all believers.  
You are seated at God's right hand in glory.  
We believe that you will come and be our judge.  
&emsp;Come then, Lord, and help your people,  
&emsp;bought with the price of your own blood,  
&emsp;and bring us with your saints  
&emsp;to glory everlasting.

## The Collects (BCP p. 81–~85)

*The Officiant then says one or more of the following Collects*

### The Collect of the Day — Thanksgiving Day (BCP p. ~175)

**Officiant:** Almighty and gracious Father, we give you thanks for the fruits of the earth in their season and for the labors of those who harvest them. Make us, we pray, faithful stewards of your great bounty, for the provision of our necessities and the relief of all who are in need, to the glory of your Name; through Jesus Christ our Lord, who lives and reigns with you and the Holy Spirit, one God, now and for ever. Amen.

### A Collect for Peace (BCP p. ~82)

**Officiant:** O God, the author of peace and lover of concord, to know you is eternal life and to serve you is perfect freedom: Defend us, your humble servants, in all assaults of our enemies; that we, surely trusting in your defense, may not fear the power of any adversaries; through the might of Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

### A Collect for Grace (BCP p. ~83)

**Officiant:** Lord God, almighty and everlasting Father, you have brought us in safety to this new day: Preserve us with your mighty power, that we may not fall into sin, nor be overcome by adversity; and in all we do, direct us to the fulfilling of your purpose; through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

## The General Thanksgiving (BCP p. ~84)

*Before the close of the Office one or both of the following may be used*

**Officiant and People:**

Almighty God, Father of all mercies,  
we your unworthy servants give you humble thanks  
for all your goodness and loving-kindness  
to us and to all whom you have made.  
We bless you for our creation, preservation,  
and all the blessings of this life;  
but above all for your immeasurable love  
in the redemption of the world by our Lord Jesus Christ;  
for the means of grace, and for the hope of glory.  
And, we pray, give us such an awareness of your mercies,  
that with truly thankful hearts we may show forth your praise,  
not only with our lips, but in our lives,  
by giving up our selves to your service,  
and by walking before you  
in holiness and righteousness all our days;  
through Jesus Christ our Lord,  
to whom, with you and the Holy Spirit,  
be honor and glory throughout all ages. Amen.  

### A Prayer of St. Chrysostom (BCP p. ~85)

**Officiant:** Almighty God, you have given us grace at this time with one accord to make our common supplication to you; and you have promised through your well-beloved Son that when two or three are gathered together in his Name you will be in the midst of them: Fulfill now, O Lord, our desires and petitions as may be best for us; granting us in this world knowledge of your truth, and in the age to come life everlasting. Amen.
