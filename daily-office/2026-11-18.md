*The following Confession of Sin may then be said; or the Office may continue at once with “Lord, open our lips.”*

## The Lessons (BCP p. 84)

### The First Lesson: Malachi 1:1, 6–14

**Reader:** A Reading from the Book of the prophet Malachi.

*In brief:* Malachi, whose name means 'my messenger,' delivers an oracle in which the Lord asks his priests: if I am a father, where is my honor? If I am a master, where is my respect? They have offered blind and lame animals on the altar, gifts they would never dare give their governor. The Lord declares that his name will be great among the nations, with pure offerings from the rising of the sun to its setting, and rebukes those who cheat him by pledging a fine animal and then offering a blemished one.

*The full NRSV text is in your Bible or an authorized source:* [Malachi 1:1, 6–14 (NRSV)](https://bible.oremus.org/?passage=Malachi+1%3A1%2C6-14&version=nrsv)

**Reader:** The Word of the Lord.  
**People:** **Thanks be to God.**

*Silence may be kept.*

Your gates will always be open; *  
&emsp;by day or night they will never be shut.

They will call you, The City of the Lord, *  
&emsp;The Zion of the Holy One of Israel.

&emsp;and your God will be your glory.

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit: *  
&emsp;as it was in the beginning, is now, and will be for ever. Amen.

### The Second Lesson: Luke 17:11–19

**Reader:** A Reading from the Gospel according to Luke.

*In brief:* On the way to Jerusalem, ten lepers call out to Jesus for mercy, and he sends them to show themselves to the priests. As they go they are made clean. One of them, a Samaritan, turns back, praising God aloud and falling at Jesus' feet to give thanks. Jesus asks where the other nine are, and says to him, 'Get up and go on your way; your faith has made you well.'

*The full NRSV text is in your Bible or an authorized source:* [Luke 17:11–19 (NRSV)](https://bible.oremus.org/?passage=Luke+17%3A11-19&version=nrsv)

**Reader:** The Word of the Lord.  
**People:** **Thanks be to God.**

*Silence may be kept.*

## The Collects (BCP p. 98–100)

*The Officiant then says one or more of the following Collects*

### The Collect of the Day — Proper 28 (BCP p. 236)

**Officiant:** Blessed Lord, who caused all holy Scriptures to be written for our learning: Grant us so to hear them, read, mark, learn, and inwardly digest them, that we may embrace and ever hold fast the blessed hope of everlasting life, which you have given us in our Savior Jesus Christ; who lives and reigns with you and the Holy Spirit, one God, for ever and ever. ### A Collect for Peace (BCP p. 99)

**Officiant:** O God, the author of peace and lover of concord, to know you is eternal life and to serve you is perfect freedom: Defend us, your humble servants, in all assaults of our enemies; that we, surely trusting in your defense, may not fear the power of any adversaries; through the might of Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

Amen.

## The General Thanksgiving (BCP p. 101)

*Before the close of the Office one or both of the following may be used*

**Officiant and People:**

Almighty God, Father of all mercies,  
we your unworthy servants give you humble thanks  
for all your goodness and loving-kindness  
to us and to all whom you have made.  
We bless you for our creation, preservation,  
and all the blessings of this life;  
but above all for your immeasurable love  
in the redemption of the world by our Lord Jesus Christ;  
for the means of grace, and for the hope of glory.  
And, we pray, give us such an awareness of your mercies,  
that with truly thankful hearts we may show forth your praise,  
not only with our lips, but in our lives,  
by giving up our selves to your service,  
and by walking before you  
in holiness and righteousness all our days;  
through Jesus Christ our Lord,  
to whom, with you and the Holy Spirit,  
be honor and glory throughout all ages. Amen.
