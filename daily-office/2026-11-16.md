### The First Lesson: Habakkuk 2:1–4, 9–20

**Reader:** A Reading from the Book of the prophet Habakkuk.

*In brief:* Habakkuk takes his stand on the watchtower to wait for God’s answer to his complaint. The Lord tells him to write the vision down plainly, because it will surely come at its appointed time: the proud will not endure, but the righteous live by their faith. Then come a series of woes against those who build their houses on plunder, found their towns on bloodshed, disgrace their neighbors, and trust in idols that cannot speak or breathe. The lesson ends in awe: the Lord is in his holy temple, and all the earth is to keep silence before him.

*The full NRSV text is in your Bible or an authorized source:* [Habakkuk 2:1–4, 9–20 (NRSV)](https://bible.oremus.org/?passage=Habakkuk+2%3A1-4%2C9-20&version=nrsv)

**Reader:** The Word of the Lord.  
**People:** **Thanks be to God.**

*Silence may be kept.*

### The Second Lesson: Luke 16:19–31

**Reader:** A Reading from the Gospel according to Luke.

*In brief:* Jesus tells of a rich man who feasted every day while a poor man named Lazarus lay hungry and covered with sores at his gate. When both die, Lazarus is carried to Abraham’s side and the rich man finds himself in torment. He begs for relief, and then asks that Lazarus be sent to warn his five brothers. Abraham answers that a great chasm is fixed between them, and that anyone who will not listen to Moses and the prophets will not be convinced even by someone who rises from the dead.

*The full NRSV text is in your Bible or an authorized source:* [Luke 16:19–31 (NRSV)](https://bible.oremus.org/?passage=Luke+16%3A19-31&version=nrsv)

**Reader:** The Word of the Lord.  
**People:** **Thanks be to God.**

*Silence may be kept.*

## The General Thanksgiving (BCP p. 101)

*Before the close of the Office one or both of the following may be used*

**Officiant and People:**

Almighty God, Father of all mercies,  
we your unworthy servants give you humble thanks  
for all your goodness and loving-kindness  
to us and to all whom you have made.  
We bless you for our creation, preservation,  
and all the blessings of this life;  
but above all for your immeasurable love  
in the redemption of the world by our Lord Jesus Christ;  
for the means of grace, and for the hope of glory.  
And, we pray, give us such an awareness of your mercies,  
that with truly thankful hearts we may show forth your praise,  
not only with our lips, but in our lives,  
by giving up our selves to your service,  
and by walking before you  
in holiness and righteousness all our days;  
through Jesus Christ our Lord,  
to whom, with you and the Holy Spirit,  
be honor and glory throughout all ages. Amen.
