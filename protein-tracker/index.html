Why this matters

At ages 68–71, muscles respond less efficiently to protein (anabolic resistance). Experts recommend 1.0–1.5 g protein per kg of body weight daily (default target 1.2 g/kg) to help preserve muscle, strength, and support brain health. One meal a day makes it harder to hit the total and to stimulate muscle-building multiple times. Pairing higher protein with even light resistance training (bodyweight, bands, or weights 2–3×/week) is the strongest way to maintain independence and function.

    Google Sheet backend (optional)

    By default data is stored only in this browser (localStorage). To share between devices or people:

    1. Create a Google Sheet with columns: Date, Person, WeightKg, ProteinG, Notes, Timestamp
    2. Publish it to the web as CSV, or create a Google Apps Script web app that accepts POST for append and GET for read.
    3. Paste the CSV URL or Apps Script endpoint below and reload.

    Config is also stored locally. For full write support use an Apps Script web app that returns JSON.
