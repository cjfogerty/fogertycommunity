# Field & Logistics Notes

**Sports Park**  
3589 Hwy K, O'Fallon, MO 63368

U4/5 practices and games use **Field 8**, which is subdivided into smaller fields labeled **A, B, C, D**, etc.

- **Practices:** Field **8C** — Wednesdays 5:30–6:00 PM
- **Games:** Field **8B** — Saturdays (U5 Girls league)

Parking is plentiful. Look for the soccer complex area. Restrooms and (during games) concessions are available.

Field status / rainouts: Always check Statusfy before leaving home.
