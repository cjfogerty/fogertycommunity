# Extra Practice Ideas & Games for U5

### Ball Mastery
- Toe taps (lightly tap the top of the ball with alternating feet)
- Sole rolls (roll the ball forward/back with the bottom of the foot)
- Inside/outside cuts while walking

### Games
- **Red Light / Green Light** – Classic, with a ball
- **Sharks and Minnows** – One or two "sharks" try to kick balls away; minnows dribble across
- **Treasure Hunt** – Cones scattered; kids dribble to a cone, pick it up, bring it back while dribbling
- **Freeze Tag** – If tagged, freeze with ball; teammates unfreeze by touching
- **Follow the Leader** – Coach or parent dribbles, kids copy

### Coaching Philosophy Reminder
- Demonstrate more than talk
- Keep instructions under 15–20 seconds
- Change activities every 5–8 minutes
- High energy and silly is better than perfect technique
- End every session with a team cheer
