Update the export

The page reads stepup-watchdog/checks.json in the browser. No build, no keys, no private APIs. Replace days and members when a new StepUp group export comes in, then commit and refresh after GitHub Pages updates.

  1. days is the date columns, oldest first: 2026-10-01 through the last day.
  2. One object per person inside members. days on that person lines up with those dates. Use null where the export says N.A.
  3. totalSteps, avgDailySteps, dailyGoal, and totalDistanceMi are the export totals, not something the page invents.
  4. Watch lines live under watch. The page marks a day at or above bigDay, and a day at or above catchUpMin that follows a day under catchUpPriorMax.
  5. The note under the highlighted person is computed in the browser. Logged days under slump.floor are replaced with the mean of that person’s other logged days. It is an estimate, and it is not written into totalSteps.
